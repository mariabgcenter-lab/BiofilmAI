🔬 Showcased Conceptual AI Frameworks
This portfolio highlights a series of innovative computational research initiatives designed to bridge raw microbiology wet-lab assays with advanced machine learning [1]:

1. 📸 BiofilmNN — AI for Microbial Imaging
Overview: A conceptual deep‑learning system designed to analyze Confocal Laser Scanning Microscopy (CLSM), Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM), and fluorescence images [3].
Scientific Core: Investigates how neural networks can support microbial imaging workflows by extracting diagnostic morphological phenotypes directly from pixel‑level biological data [3].
2. 🧪 Automated PCR Optimizer — AI for Molecular Assay Development
Overview: A Python‑based concept tool utilizing Bayesian optimization algorithms [3].
Scientific Core: Explores how AI can systematically streamline PCR protocol development, reduce validation timelines, improve assay robustness, and support molecular diagnostics across diverse workflows [3].
3. 🧬 AMR Prediction Framework — AI for Antimicrobial Resistance Diagnostics
Overview: A proposed multimodal AI model integrating multiple diagnostic data types [5].
Scientific Core: Explores how combining Fluorescence In Situ Hybridization (FISH)-based imaging and raw gene expression profiles can improve early AMR detection and support clinical decision‑making [5].
4. 📊 Microbiome Visualization Tools
Overview: A planned suite of AI‑assisted interactive dashboards [5].
Scientific Core: Focuses on making complex polymicrobial community dynamics interpretable through interactive visualization architectures, supporting ecological modeling and deeper diagnostic insights [5].
